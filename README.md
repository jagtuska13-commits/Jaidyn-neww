# Stratagem

A jurisdiction-aware legal AI for your pocket: analyze any legal document, fine, bill, contract, or question — with live-searched, current law for your specific area and date.

- **Legal chat** powered by Mistral AI with live web search for current statutes and precedents
- **Document analysis** — upload contracts or PDFs; OCR extracts the full text for analysis
- **Jurisdiction awareness** — set your area once; every answer applies the law of that jurisdiction as in force today
- **Voice cloning + text-to-speech** — replies spoken aloud, optionally in your own voice
- **Dispute case tracking** — snap a bill or fine, get a legally-grounded dispute letter, track it to resolution
- **Music Studio** — AI songwriting with on-device synth and optional pro AI vocals
- **Persistent memory** — learns you across sessions; persona and profile injection

No Google accounts, no Google APIs. One free Mistral API key is all you need.

## Setup

Add a `MISTRAL_API_KEY` secret to GitHub Actions; the cloud build produces a signed APK release automatically.
